Spiced Lamb Flatbread

This flatbread was inspired by a tiny street-side stall we stumbled onto during a trip through Istanbul. The vendor worked with an economy of motion that comes only from decades of practice — stretching dough paper-thin, scattering seasoned lamb across it, and sliding it into a wood-fired oven in one fluid gesture.

We stood in the cold evening air eating it with our hands, the bread still crackling, the lamb impossibly fragrant. It was one of those meals that changes what you think food can be.

This version is our attempt to bring that memory home. The flatbread is simple — just flour, water, oil, and salt — and the spiced lamb comes together quickly. It won't be exactly the same as that Istanbul evening, but it gets close enough to make you smile.

The Recipe

Ingredients

Flatbread

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3/4 cup warm water

Spiced Lamb

  • 500g ground lamb
  • 1 medium onion, finely diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp coriander
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp chili flakes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • Salt and pepper to taste

To Serve

  • 1/2 cup Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • Fresh parsley and mint
  • Sumac for sprinkling

Instructions

  1. Make the dough: Combine flour and salt. Add olive oil and warm water, mixing until a shaggy dough forms. Knead for 5 minutes until smooth and elastic. Cover and rest 30 minutes.
  2. Cook the lamb: Heat a large skillet over medium-high. Cook onion until softened, about 5 minutes. Add garlic and spices, stirring for 1 minute until fragrant. Add the lamb, breaking it up, and cook until browned. Stir in tomato paste, season with salt and pepper, and set aside.
  3. Make the tahini yogurt: Whisk together yogurt, tahini, and lemon juice. Season with a pinch of salt.
  4. Roll and cook flatbreads: Divide dough into 4 pieces. Roll each very thin. Cook in a dry, screaming-hot skillet for 1–2 minutes per side until blistered and puffed.
  5. Assemble: Spread tahini yogurt on warm flatbread, top with spiced lamb, scatter with fresh herbs and a dusting of sumac. Serve immediately.
