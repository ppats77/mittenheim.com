Holiday Mulled Wine with Goji Berries

There's a moment every winter when the cold stops being charming and starts being personal. That's when you make mulled wine. Not because it's fancy, but because standing over a pot of simmering spiced wine while the whole kitchen fills with cinnamon and orange is the closest thing to a hug you can get from a stove.

This version leans into the warm spices — cinnamon, cloves, star anise, cardamom, fresh ginger — and adds apple cider for a little sweetness and body. The goji berries are a quiet twist: they plump up in the warm wine and add a subtle tartness that keeps everything from tipping into dessert territory.

You can simmer it for 15 minutes and call it done, or let it go low and slow for up to 3 hours if you want the spices to really settle in. Either way, ladle it into glasses, drop in a cinnamon stick and a star anise, and settle in for the evening.

The Recipe

Serves: 4–6  •  Prep: 10 min  •  Cook: 15 min  •  Total: 25 min

Ingredients

  • 1 (750ml) bottle red wine
  • 2 cups organic, no sugar added, apple cider
  • 2–4 tablespoons honey, to your taste
  • 4 cinnamon sticks, plus more for serving
  • 4 cloves
  • 2 star anise, plus more for serving
  • 1 tablespoon cardamom pods
  • 1 inch fresh ginger, sliced
  • 1 orange, sliced, plus more for serving
  • 1/2 cup dried goji berries

Instructions

  1. Simmer: In a medium saucepan, combine all ingredients and bring to a simmer over low heat. Simmer 15–20 minutes or up to 3 hours. If desired, strain out the spices and fruits.
  2. Serve: Divide the wine among glasses. Garnish with orange slices, cinnamon sticks, and star anise.
