# Our Favorite Weeknight Curry

**Golubka Kitchen**

---

> *The umami from the tomatoes, mixed with the rich fattiness of coconut milk, makes for the silkiest, most flavorful curry base, with minimal effort.*

---

**Serves:** 6
**Type:** Vegan, One-Pot
**Time:** ~35 minutes

---

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient |
|--------|------------|
| 1 tablespoon | coconut oil |
| 1 | yellow onion, chopped |
| 2-3 cloves | garlic, minced |
| 2 teaspoons | curry powder |
| 1 tablespoon | tomato paste |
| 1 (28 oz) can | crushed tomatoes (fire-roasted work well) |
| 1 (13.5 oz) can | unsweetened coconut milk |
| 1 head | cauliflower, chopped into bite-sized florets |
| 1 | red bell pepper, sliced into bite-sized ribbons |
| 1 (14 oz) block | tofu, dried with a towel and cubed |
| 1 large bunch | spinach (~4 packed cups), roughly chopped |

---

## Instructions

**1.** Melt the coconut oil in a soup pot over **medium heat**. Add the onion and saut&eacute; for about **7 minutes**, until translucent. Add the garlic and stir for about **30 seconds**, until fragrant. Add the curry powder and mix it in, then add the tomato paste and work it in with your spatula.

**2.** Add the crushed tomatoes, coconut milk, cauliflower, bell pepper, and tofu. Mix everything to coat. Turn the heat up to **high** and bring to a boil. Lower the heat to a **simmer** and cook, **covered**, for about **20 minutes**, or until the cauliflower is soft and cooked through.

**3.** Turn off the heat and stir in the spinach to wilt.

**4.** Serve over rice or any grain of choice, with a squeeze of fresh lime.

---

## Notes

- Load it up with whatever veggies are in season or on hand.
- The base of canned tomatoes + coconut milk is the key -- rich, silky, and full of flavor.
- Cauliflower adds cruciferous power, red bell pepper rounds it out with sweetness, and spinach brings the greens.
- The tofu makes it fully satisfying as a complete meal.
