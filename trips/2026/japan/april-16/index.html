10:45 Tokyo Tower
🗼 Tokyo Tower (東京タワー)
4-2-8 Shibakoen, Minato City · 9 AM–11 PM
Main Deck (150m): ¥1,500 adult · ¥1,000 child (12–15) · ¥700 child (4–11)
Total for 4 (Main Deck only): ~¥5,500

Why visit:

  • Tokyo’s iconic 1958 Eiffel-style tower — symbol of post-war Japan
  • 333m tall, “international orange” for aviation safety
  • Main Deck has 360° views + glass floor section
  • More retro and architecturally beautiful than Skytree
  • Great views of Tokyo Bay, Mt. Fuji on clear days

Tips:

  • Buy tickets at counter on arrival (no advance reservation needed)
  • Aim for ~45 min to 1 hour at the tower
  • Skip the Top Deck (¥3,500) — Main Deck is plenty after Skytree

Quick facts:

  • Built 1958 by Tachū Naitō · 333m (taller than Eiffel Tower at 330m)
  • “International Orange” + white per aviation safety law
  • Appeared in countless films, anime, and manga as Tokyo’s symbol

16:40 Dinner — Tensan Tempura 💰 CASH ONLY
🍤 Tensan (天三)
Motoasakusa 2-8-8, Taito City · Inaricho area
Thu hours: 11:30 AM–2 PM / 5 PM–8 PM
⚠️ CASH ONLY — bring ¥10,000–15,000 for 4
⚠️ NO RESERVATIONS — walk-in only, ~13 seats (2 tables of 4 + 5 counter)

Strategy:

  1. Arrive by 4:40 PM — 20 min before opening
  2. Sign the clipboard list outside the door
  3. Be one of the first 4 groups to guarantee a table for 4
  4. 5:00 PM seated

What to order:

  • Tendon (天井) — tempura over rice bowl, hearty
  • Ten-teishoku (天定食) — tempura set meal with rice, miso, pickles — more variety
  • Anago tempura — conger eel, seasonal specialty
  • Shrimp + vegetable + kisu (whiting) tempura assortment

Why it’s special:

  • Long-established Edo-style tempura restaurant
  • Light, crispy, fried fresh in front of you
  • Authentic neighborhood spot, not a tourist chain
  • Tabelog rated, locally beloved

About Edo-style tempura:

Classic Tokyo-style from the 1700s — shogunate-era street vendors sold fried seafood to samurai and townspeople.

  • Sesame oil base (not pure vegetable oil) — darker color, richer aroma
  • Lighter batter — focus is on the ingredient, not the coating
  • Fresh seafood from Tokyo Bay — shrimp, kisu, anago, squid
  • Tentsuyu dipping sauce — soy + dashi + mirin + grated daikon
  • Salt option — for purists, dipped in flavored salt instead

Watch the chef fry each piece individually — it’s an art form.

18:30 Senso-ji Temple at Night
⛩️ Senso-ji (浅草寺)
2-3-1 Asakusa, Taito City · Always open (free) · ~10 min walk north from Tensan

Why visit at night:

  • Tokyo’s oldest and most famous temple (founded 645 CE)
  • Lit up beautifully after dark — the giant red lantern at Kaminarimon glows, the pagoda is illuminated
  • Way fewer tourists than daytime
  • More magical atmosphere — quieter, contemplative

What to see (~45 min):

  • 🏮 Kaminarimon Gate — iconic giant red lantern photo
  • 🛒️ Nakamise-dori Street — shops closed but lit up beautifully
  • ⛩️ Main Hall (Hondo) — illuminated, visible from outside
  • 🗼 5-story pagoda — beautifully lit at night
  • 🌃 Tokyo Skytree visible in the distance
