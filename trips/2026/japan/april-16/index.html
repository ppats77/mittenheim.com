10:45 Tokyo Tower
🗼 Tokyo Tower (東京タワー)
4-2-8 Shibakoen, Minato City · 9 AM–11 PM
Main Deck (150m): ¥1,500 adult · ¥1,000 child (12–15) · ¥700 child (4–11)
Total for 4 (Main Deck only): ~¥5,500

Why visit:

  • Tokyo’s iconic 1958 Eiffel-style tower — symbol of post-war Japan
  • 333m tall, “international orange” for aviation safety
  • Main Deck has 360° views + glass floor section
  • More retro and architecturally beautiful than Skytree
  • Great views of Tokyo Bay, Mt. Fuji on clear days

Tips:

  • Buy tickets at counter on arrival (no advance reservation needed)
  • Aim for ~45 min to 1 hour at the tower
  • Skip the Top Deck (¥3,500) — Main Deck is plenty after Skytree

Quick facts:

  • Built 1958 by Tachū Naitō · 333m (taller than Eiffel Tower at 330m)
  • “International Orange” + white per aviation safety law
  • Appeared in countless films, anime, and manga as Tokyo’s symbol
📍 Tokyo Tower
11:45 Zojo-ji Temple + Iconic Photo
⛩️ Zojo-ji (増上寺)
4-7-35 Shibakoen, Minato City · Always open (free) · Main hall 9 AM–5 PM

Why it’s iconic:

  • Founded 1393, family temple of the Tokugawa shoguns
  • The signature photo: Zojo-ji’s traditional roof framed by Tokyo Tower behind it — one of Tokyo’s most famous views
  • 6 Tokugawa shogun graves on the grounds
  • Beautiful Sangedatsumon main gate (1622, designated cultural property)

Photo spot: Stand in front of the main hall, frame the temple roof with Tokyo Tower rising directly behind it. ~15 min.

Thu hours: 11:30 AM–3 PM / 4 PM–midnight (last food 11 PM)

Why it’s a great last-night dinner:

  • Juicy handmade gyoza — specially developed skin, hand-wrapped by skilled artisans
  • Famous for nikujiru (meat-juice) gyoza that bursts with broth when you bite in
  • Chicken wing gyoza, beer pairing, all-you-can-drink courses available
  • Big, lively gyoza izakaya — perfect family-friendly last-night vibe
  • 15-min walk back to hotel after dinner — no trains, no rush

What to order:

  • Nikujiru Gyoza (meat-juice gyoza) — the signature, must-order
  • Yaki gyoza assortment — grilled variety platter
  • Chicken wing gyoza — stuffed chicken wings, a house specialty
  • Kimchi, edamame, fried rice, draft beer for the grownups
