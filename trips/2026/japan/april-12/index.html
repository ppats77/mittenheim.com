18:00 Tokyo Skytree Entry
🎫 Entry Window: 18:00–18:29 JST
Booking: ARS_SKYTREE_LINK-20260407-Y3MB · Name: Jenssen Maxim
Tembo Deck (350m) + Tembo Galleria (450m) · 3 Adults + 1 Child · ¥14,000

Entry Instructions:

  1. Go to 4F Ticket Counter (Sky Arena)
  2. Present the 2D barcode on your phone
  3. Make sure phone has internet — voucher loads via web
  4. High-speed elevator goes up at ~600m/min!

Tembo Deck (350m)

  • 360° views, café, glass floor section
  • Head WEST for sunset at 18:11
  • Clear days: Mt. Fuji, Tokyo Tower, Shinjuku, Tokyo Bay

Tembo Galleria (450m)

  • Spiraling skywalk — highest accessible point in Tokyo
  • Sorakara Point at 451.2m — the absolute top
  • Stay 30+ min during blue hour (18:30–19:30) for best city lights
