Wednesday, July 8 Day Plan 🏖️ OFF

East Berlin day — the Wall, DDR & Karl-Marx-Allee

← PrevNext →

🚩 The Soviet-flavour day. Real Wall with death strip and watchtower in the morning, DDR everyday life hands-on in the afternoon, Stalin-era boulevard before dinner. Almost the whole day rides on the U2 + tram M10 — the M10 follows the old border strip.

TimeWhat
9:00Breakfast at Winterfeldtmarkt (Wed market, 5 min walk from hotel) — pastries, fruit, people-watching on Berlin’s prettiest square
10:00U2 → Potsdamer Platz, change to S1/S2 → Nordbahnhof (~25 min)
10:15Berlin Wall Memorial, Bernauer Straße (free): preserved death strip, watchtower, Window of Remembrance, Documentation Center rooftop view
12:15Tram M10 (the “Wall tram”) → Eberswalder Straße
12:30Lunch: Konnopke’s Imbiss under the U2 viaduct — East Berlin’s Currywurst institution since 1930 (the DDR classic is “ohne Darm”, skinless)
14:00U2 Eberswalder Str. → Alexanderplatz (direct): Weltzeituhr world clock, TV Tower from below — East Berlin’s showpiece square
14:30DDR Museum (~2 h, booked slot): drive the Trabi simulator, walk through a full DDR flat, open every drawer
17:00U5 one stop → Strausberger Platz: walk Karl-Marx-Allee — Stalin’s “workers’ palaces”, tiled facades, Kino International — up to the Frankfurter Tor towers
19:30Dinner: Zur letzten Instanz (reserved?) — Berlin’s oldest restaurant, est. 1621: Königsberger Klopse, Buletten, Eisbein. Napoleon and Gorbachev both ate here
21:30U2 Klosterstraße → Nollendorfplatz, direct home

🌦️ Plan B / extras: if the weather is kind and nobody is museum-tired, Prater Garten (Berlin’s oldest beer garden, 1837, Kastanienallee) is 5 min from Konnopke’s. Fernsehturm ride up (203 m) is a fun add-on at Alexanderplatz but needs a pre-booked slot — skip if the queue is silly, the Reichstag dome tomorrow is free and arguably better.

📖 Today&rsquo;s backstories

🧱 The Berlin Wall Built overnight in 1961, it cut a city in half for 28 years — and fell because of a bungled press conference. 🚗 Everyday life in the DDR Ten-year waiting lists for a cardboard car, one informer per ninety citizens, and the little traffic-light man who survived it all. 🚩 Karl-Marx-Allee Stalin’s showcase boulevard — “workers’ palaces” in wedding-cake style, and the street where the 1953 uprising began. 🌭 Berlin on a plate Currywurst was invented with bartered ketchup in 1949; the Döner kebab as we know it was born at Bahnhof Zoo in 1972.
